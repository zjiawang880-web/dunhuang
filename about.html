THE ANCIENT SILK ROAD

OUR PROJECT

Why Dunhuang?

Dunhuang is known for its history, art, and important role along the ancient Silk Road. Through this project, we hope to make information about Dunhuang easier to explore. Our website brings together cultural stories, travel guides, and interactive experiences to help visitors discover Dunhuang in a more enjoyable way.
