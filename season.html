EXPLORE THE SEASONS

Four Seasons in Dunhuang

Every season offers a different way to explore Dunhuang.

🌸

Spring

March to May. Comfortable daytime temperatures, cool mornings and occasional wind.

☀️

Summer

June to August. Bright sunshine, long days and hot afternoons.

🍂

Autumn

September to November. Clear skies, comfortable weather and cool nights.

❄️

Winter

December to February. Very cold, dry weather and fewer visitors.

TRAVEL PLANNING

The Best Time to Explore

April to June and September to October are often comfortable periods for visiting.

🌤 Comfortable Weather

Spring and autumn are generally suitable for walking and sightseeing.

🎫 Plan Ahead

Book Mogao Caves tickets early, especially during holidays.

🧥 Prepare Your Clothes

Dunhuang has large differences between daytime and nighttime temperatures.

BEFORE YOU GO

Travel Tips

☀️ Sun Protection

Bring sunscreen, sunglasses and a hat when exploring outdoor attractions.

💧 Stay Hydrated

Dunhuang is very dry. Carry drinking water during your trip.

🌙 Be Ready for Cool Nights

Temperatures can fall quickly at night. Pack an extra layer.
